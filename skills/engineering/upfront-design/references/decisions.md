# ADR offers

Only after a choice passes all three entry-file tests: hard to reverse,
surprising without context, chosen over a real alternative.
Qualifying choices include architectural/integration shape, costly technology,
deliberate boundary exclusions, deviations, and non-obvious rejected alternatives.

On user confirmation, write in the owning repo's discovered ADR tree; create
docs/adr lazily if absent. Scan the highest NNNN prefix, increment it, and name
`NNNN-<slug>.md`. Use a short title and one to three sentences covering context,
decision, and reason. Add Status, Considered Options, or Consequences only when
they carry information those sentences lack. Record declined offers too.
