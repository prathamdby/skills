## Kind

One kind of text per section. A fact, an argument, and an instruction do not share a paragraph. Headings the leaf already requires assign the kind.

## Length

An instruction is under 20 words. Any other sentence is under 25 words. A required opening, a path, a SHA, a quoted error, or a code literal is exempt. A character cap in the leaf wins over the word cap.

## Words

"Only" and "not" sit next to the word they change. A noun string longer than three words is broken. Every "it", "this", and "they" points at one noun.

## Punctuation

No semicolons, slashes, "(s)", Latin abbreviations, or em dashes. Use periods or commas. A path, a SHA, a quoted error, a code literal, or a required opening may contain those characters. The slash ban forbids a construction such as "and/or", not a separator inside a path.

## Names

One word per action. One name per thing. Use active voice with a named actor. A commit subject and a PR title stay imperative. Do not add an actor to those titles.

## Address

"You" only in an instruction to a person. A commit message, a PR title, and a PR body stay impersonal facts. Do not address that reader as "you".

AI vocabulary means additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, abstract landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, and vibrant.
